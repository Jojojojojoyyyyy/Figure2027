Part 3 · Nutrition

Fuel to build

Protein stays at your current ~140 g — the added calories come from carbs, on top. That's the lever for more muscle.

Gout / uric-acid: lean on the low-purine backbone — whey, egg whites, dairy, whole egg. Treat tofu/tempeh/legumes as rotation (spread across the day) and mushrooms as a vegetable. Hydrate, but ~3.5–4.5 L is plenty.

Part 5 · Running

Monthly calendar to 10K

Gentle build from your real 2 km base — the 4 km start was too high. Run each session in its colour-coded zone; never start a run faster than easy.

Z2 Easy / Long · 60–70% HRmax (~113–132 bpm) · ~7:30/km Z3 Tempo · 70–80% (~132–150 bpm) · ~6:00–6:30/km Z4 Intervals · 80–90% (~150–170 bpm) · ~5:30/km Lift = strength day (grey)
Starts at your current 2 km and builds gently (~10–15%/week): weekly volume ~8 → 10 → 13 → 14 km, peaking at an 8–10 km test on the final Saturday — run-walk the last stretch if needed. Keep long runs away from Mon/Sun leg & posterior days; fuel your runs. If 10 km isn't comfortable yet, repeat weeks 3–4 once before pushing.
